Fish Recognizer

What This Project Does

I made this tool so that anyone can identify a fish they catch. Many people do not know the name of the fish they pull out of the water. This page gives them a fast way to find out what they caught.

How To Use It

1. Press Start to turn on your camera. 2. Hold the fish in front of the camera. 3. The model will read the image and show the name of the fish along with a score. Higher scores mean a stronger match.
