Fish Recognizer

What This Project Does

I made this tool so that I could identify a fish that I caught. Many people enjoy fishing but do not always know what species they pulled from the water. This project gives a fast and simple way to identify fish using a camera.

How To Use It

1. Press Start to turn on your camera.
2. Hold the fish in front of the camera.
3. The model will display the fish name with a confidence score.
